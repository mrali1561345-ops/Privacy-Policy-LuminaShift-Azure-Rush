# Privacy Policy - LuminaShift Azure Rush

**Last updated:** October 2026

**LuminaShift: Azure Rush** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates regarding user data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**LuminaShift: Azure Rush** is built as an offline-capable arcade game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

## 2. Local Game Storage
The app saves non-personal game statistics locally on your mobile device, including:
* High Scores
* In-game currency (Azure Coins)
* Unlocked core skins and selected preferences (Audio, Haptics, Difficulty)

This data remains stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The application does not integrate third-party tracking software, advertising networks, or analytics frameworks that gather user data.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
